## 🔬 How I Approach Problems

1. Understand the actual business impact first.
2. Break the problem into the smallest testable pieces.
3. Build incrementally.
4. Add logging and monitoring early, not after something breaks.
5. Refactor once the shape of the problem is actually clear.

I'd rather ship something simple and observable than something clever and fragile.
